## Notes for the reviewing lawyer (not part of the documents)

1. **The Spanish version governs.** The Terms already say that where the Spanish and any
   translation disagree, the Spanish prevails. That clause is carried into the English below,
   so the English is a reading aid with a stated hierarchy, not a second contract.
2. **Jurisdiction** is the courts of the user's own domicile, which travels well to a
   non-Spanish consumer. Governing law is Spanish throughout. Worth a second look for a
   United States user, since that is a choice, not a translation question.
3. **The Pro subscription** is described in the Terms as launching September 2026 at
   14,99 EUR monthly and 89,99 EUR yearly. There is no purchase of any kind in the app today.
   Nothing is mis-sold, but the paragraph describes something that does not exist yet.
4. **Drafting slip in the Spanish Terms:** the heading "Edad mínima" appears twice, the second
   time with no text under it. Not reproduced below.
5. **Data protection references** are GDPR, the Spanish data protection act and the Spanish
   supervisory authority. Correct for the EU. A United States user is pointed to a Spanish
   authority, which is accurate but may want a sentence acknowledging it.
