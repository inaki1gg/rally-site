1.THE APPLICATION

1.1What the RALLY App is

RALLY is a digital platform accessible through a website and mobile devices that allows registered users to:

  • Record the results of tennis and/or padel matches
  • Generate player rankings and ratings
  • View sporting statistics and performance
  • Access activity histories and sporting progression
  • Connect with friends and friends of friends and view friends' rankings
  • Mark availability and create or join open matches
  • Communicate with the other players in a match via match chat
  • Take part in free or paid leagues and competitions, including open competitions such as the Cold Open
  • Search for other players by name or username to log a match or choose a competition partner
  • See friends' matches in a feed and recognise them with "props"

The rankings, ratings and statistics generated by the Application are purely recreational and informative in nature and have no official status, unless expressly stated otherwise.

RALLY's services are currently aimed at players who play in Madrid (Spain).

1.2Compatibility

The Application is compatible with the iOS and Android operating systems. For the Application to work correctly, users are advised to update their operating system to the most recent versions. You can download the Application from Apple's App Store or from Android's Play Store. The Website may be accessed using compatible browsers and an internet connection.

Creating a user account is required in order to use certain features. During registration, users must provide truthful, accurate and up-to-date information, including, among others: Name and surname, Email, Username, Sport (padel and/or tennis), City of residence, and answers to the level questionnaire (years playing, federation rating or level and similar). Gender and Date of birth may be provided optionally. The user's country is taken from their device's region setting. If the user signs up with "Sign in with Apple" or "Sign in with Google", RALLY receives the user's name and email address from that provider (for Apple, this may be a private relay address).

Users are responsible for keeping their credentials confidential, for all activity carried out from their account, and for updating their information when necessary.

RALLY may suspend, restrict or delete accounts that:

1.5Account cancellation

Users may request the closure of their account at any time through the Platform itself or by contacting RALLY. Deletion of an account may entail:

2.1User-generated content, match chat and zero tolerance

RALLY has zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour between users, including in match chat.

It is expressly prohibited to publish, display or distribute through the Platform, including through match chat, any content that is offensive, obscene, discriminatory, defamatory, threatening or harassing towards other users, including usernames, display names, group names, chat messages, feed content and "props". Match chat may be used only for organising the relevant match. The following are also expressly prohibited in match chat: spam, unsolicited advertising or commercial links, sharing third parties' personal data without their consent, harassment, and persistent contact after a block.

RALLY may filter, reject or remove any user-generated content that breaches this clause.

Users have mechanisms within the Application to report messages and users and to block users. Blocking prevents any further interaction between the two users within the Platform and removes their mutual visibility, including through friends of friends. Friends of friends may be invited only for sporting purposes. A shared friend link shows the user's first name, rating and city to anyone with the link. The user's public profile, at rallyrating.app/ followed by their username, shows anyone, signed in or not, their name, username, padel rating and number of matches played. Match links show the match and the players' names to anyone who has the link. Accepted friends see the user's matches in the feed and can give them "props".

Any signed-in user may search for another user by name or username and see their rating (unless hidden) solely to log a match played together or to invite them as a competition partner. Misuse of the search function to contact or harass users is prohibited.

RALLY will review reports received and act on the reported content or account within a maximum of 24 hours, and may remove the content and permanently suspend or cancel the offending user's account. For friend and league matches, match chat messages are deleted 24 hours after the match's scheduled start time or immediately if the match is cancelled. Reported messages are retained for moderation for 12 months after resolution in accordance with the Privacy Policy.

Users may also report any incident relating to abusive content or conduct by writing to support@rallyrating.app.
