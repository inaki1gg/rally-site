How it works

Your rating,
earned on court.

Three steps. Zero ambiguity. Every match moves your number — for better or worse.

01

Log your match

Record the result after every game — singles or doubles, padel or tennis. Under thirty seconds. The more you log, the sharper your rating becomes.

02

Your Elo updates

Beat a stronger player and your rating climbs more. Beat a weaker one and it barely moves. Honest, dynamic, fair — the same algorithm used in chess and football rankings.

03

Know your level

See exactly where you stand on the leaderboard. Find opponents at your level. Track your progress over time with a number that means something.

Why Elo?

The same system used in chess, table tennis, and football rankings. It adjusts based on the strength of your opposition — so beating a top-rated player matters significantly more than beating a beginner. Your rating is a true reflection of your game, not just your win count.

About RALLY

Built by players,
for players.

Padel is the fastest-growing sport on the planet. In Spain alone, there are over 5 million players and thousands of clubs — yet there is still no universal way to know your level. No shared rating. No objective benchmark.

RALLY started with one question: what would tennis or padel feel like if every player carried a number that meant something? Built by two students in Madrid, obsessed with sport and data, RALLY is the performance identity layer the racket-sports world hasn't had.

We're launching with a hand-picked group of founding players who'll help shape what RALLY becomes. If you care about your game, we'd love you to be part of it.
